# Water Utility WhatsApp Bot - Ethics and Trust Framework

## Overview

This document outlines the ethical principles, data practices, and transparency measures that govern the Water Utility WhatsApp Bot service. Our commitment is to provide helpful, accurate, and trustworthy assistance while maintaining user privacy and safety.

## AI Disclosure

### What This Service Is
- **AI-Powered Assistant**: This is an artificial intelligence system designed to help with water utility-related inquiries
- **Automated Response**: Responses are generated by machine learning models, not human customer service representatives
- **Limited Scope**: The bot is specifically trained for water utility services and cannot provide general advice outside this domain

### What This Service Is Not
- **Emergency Service**: This is not an emergency response system
- **Human Agent**: You are not chatting with a human customer service representative
- **General Purpose AI**: The bot is specialized for water utility queries only

## Data Collection

### What Data We Collect
- **Conversation Content**: Your messages and the bot's responses
- **Session Metadata**: Session ID, timestamps, number of interactions
- **Performance Metrics**: Response times, intent classification confidence, resolution status
- **User Feedback**: Ratings and optional text feedback you provide
- **Technical Data**: Error logs, system performance data

### What Data We Do NOT Collect
- **Personal Identifiable Information (PII)**: Names, addresses, phone numbers are automatically redacted
- **Financial Information**: Payment details, bank account numbers
- **Location Data**: GPS coordinates or precise location information
- **Contact Lists**: Your WhatsApp contacts or other personal contacts

### Data Redaction
Our system automatically redacts:
- Full names and partial names
- Phone numbers and email addresses
- Physical addresses
- Financial account numbers
- National ID numbers
- Any other PII detected by our redaction algorithms

## Purpose of Data Collection

### Service Improvement
- **Quality Assurance**: Monitor response accuracy and helpfulness
- **Performance Optimization**: Improve response times and system reliability
- **Training Data**: Enhance the AI model's ability to understand and respond to water utility inquiries

### Operational Requirements
- **Service Delivery**: Provide accurate and timely responses to your inquiries
- **Issue Resolution**: Track complaint status and resolution progress
- **Safety Monitoring**: Detect potential safety issues or emergencies

### Analytics and Reporting
- **Service Metrics**: Generate performance reports for service improvement
- **Trend Analysis**: Identify common issues and service patterns
- **Compliance Reporting**: Meet regulatory and audit requirements

## Data Retention

### Retention Periods
- **Conversation History**: 365 days (automatically deleted after 1 year)
- **Session Metrics**: 730 days (2 years for performance analysis)
- **User Feedback**: 1095 days (3 years for service improvement)
- **Error Logs**: 90 days (3 months for system maintenance)

### Data Deletion
- **Automatic Deletion**: Data is automatically purged after retention periods
- **Manual Deletion**: Users can request immediate deletion of their conversation history
- **Emergency Deletion**: Data may be deleted immediately if safety concerns arise

## Human Intervention

### When Humans Take Over
- **Emergency Situations**: Any indication of immediate danger or emergency
- **Low Confidence**: When the AI confidence score falls below 0.3
- **Complex Issues**: Problems requiring specialized knowledge or authority
- **User Request**: When explicitly requested by the user
- **Escalation Triggers**: Multiple failed attempts to resolve an issue

### Human Agent Process
- **Notification**: User is informed when a human agent is taking over
- **Context Transfer**: Relevant conversation context is shared with human agents
- **Privacy Protection**: Only necessary information is shared with human agents
- **Follow-up**: Users can track the status of their human-assisted cases

## Limitations of AI Advice

### What We Cannot Do
- **Emergency Response**: We cannot dispatch emergency services
- **Legal Advice**: We cannot provide legal interpretations or advice
- **Medical Advice**: We cannot provide medical or health-related advice
- **Financial Counseling**: We cannot provide financial planning or investment advice
- **Guarantees**: We cannot guarantee specific outcomes or timelines

### Accuracy Limitations
- **Information Currency**: Our knowledge may not reflect the most recent changes
- **Context Understanding**: We may miss nuances or complex contextual factors
- **Technical Accuracy**: Technical information may need verification by human experts
- **Jurisdictional Differences**: Local regulations and practices may vary

### When to Verify Information
- **Critical Information**: Always verify critical safety or legal information
- **Financial Matters**: Confirm billing and payment information with official sources
- **Emergency Situations**: Contact emergency services directly
- **Complex Cases**: Request human agent assistance for complex issues

## No Hallucinated Information

### Information Sources
- **Official Documentation**: We use official water utility documentation and policies
- **Verified Data**: Information is sourced from verified utility databases
- **Standard Procedures**: Responses follow established utility procedures
- **Regulatory Compliance**: All information complies with relevant regulations

### Prevention of Hallucination
- **Source Verification**: All factual claims are verified against official sources
- **Confidence Scoring**: We indicate when we're uncertain about information
- **Human Review**: Critical information is reviewed by human agents
- **Fact Checking**: Regular audits of response accuracy

### Handling Uncertainty
- **Explicit Uncertainty**: We clearly state when information is uncertain
- **Source Attribution**: We reference official sources when available
- **Verification Recommendations**: We suggest verification for important information
- **Escalation**: We escalate when information confidence is low

- **Granular Control**: Users can choose what types of data to store

- **Withdrawal Consent**: Users can withdraw consent at any time

### Consent Types
- **Conversation Storage**: Consent to store conversation history
- **Analytics Usage**: Consent to use data for service improvement
- **Feedback Collection**: Consent to collect and use feedback
- **Human Review**: Consent for human agents to review conversations

### Consent Management
- **Clear Disclosure**: Easy-to-understand explanations of data use
- **Simple Controls**: One-click consent management
- **Transparency Reports**: Regular reports on data usage and protection

## Emergency Escalation

### Emergency Detection
- **Keyword Detection**: Automatic detection of emergency-related keywords
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Detection of urgent or distress language
- **Context Analysis**: Understanding of potentially dangerous situations
- **Pattern Recognition**: Identification of emergency conversation patterns

### Escalation Triggers
- **Safety Keywords**: Words indicating immediate danger or emergency
- **Medical Terms**: References to medical emergencies or health crises
- **Utility Emergencies**: Gas leaks, water main breaks, electrical hazards
- **Personal Safety**: Threats, violence, or personal harm indicators

### Emergency Response
- **Immediate Escalation**: Direct transfer to human emergency responders
- **Emergency Services**: Contact information for appropriate emergency services
- **Priority Handling**: Emergency requests receive immediate priority
- **Follow-up**: Post-emergency follow-up and support

## Low Confidence Escalation

### Confidence Thresholds
- **High Confidence (0.8+)**: AI handles independently
- **Medium Confidence (0.5-0.8)**: AI responds with verification suggestions
- **Low Confidence (0.3-0.5)**: AI responds with human agent offer
- **Very Low Confidence (<0.3)**: Automatic human agent escalation

### Escalation Process
- **Automatic Detection**: System detects low confidence responses
- **User Notification**: User is informed about confidence limitations
- **Human Offer**: Option to speak with a human agent is provided
- **Seamless Transfer**: Smooth transition to human agent when requested
